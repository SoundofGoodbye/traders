Positions

How to read this page

Each row is a stock you're holding (Open) or one you've already sold (Recently closed). Bought means you own it and profit if it rises; Short-sold means you profit if it falls.

For open trades the profit/loss is on paper — it moves with the price and isn't real until you sell. For closed trades it's locked in. To record a sale, type the price you got and press Sell.
