Buy-list

How to read this page

Each row is a business you'd be happy to own — and the most you'd pay for it (your target). When the latest price falls to or below your target, the row turns Ready: that's your cue to do your homework and decide. It is never an order.

Adding a name here is a reminder to wait for your price instead of reacting to the daily list. Model buy-below is this app's own rough estimate of a sensible buy price (from the company's cash earnings) — a sanity check on your target, not a recommendation.
